Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Contact details you provide (name, email, organization) when requesting a demo or contacting us.

PIPEDA & Canadian privacy

As a Canadian organization, Credproof aligns its privacy practices with PIPEDA’s fair information principles, including:

  • Accountability — a designated Privacy Officer and clear ownership of privacy practices.
  • Identifying purposes — we explain why information is collected before or at the time of collection.
  • Consent — we seek meaningful consent appropriate to the sensitivity of the information.
  • Limiting collection & use — we collect only what we need and use it for stated purposes.
  • Safeguards — technical and organizational measures to protect personal information.
  • Openness & individual access — policies are available, and individuals can request access.
  • Challenging compliance — concerns can be raised with our Privacy Officer.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Provincial private-sector laws (for example in British Columbia, Alberta, or Québec) may also apply depending on where you or our customers operate. We will update this notice as our operations expand.
