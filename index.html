What is Credproof

Digital credentials, without the lock-in

Credproof is infrastructure for verifiable credentials. Universities, banks, employers, and platforms use it to issue trusted proofs that people keep in their own wallets — then reuse across partners when they choose to share.

You get the outcomes that matter to operators and investors: fewer repeat checks, less paperwork, stronger privacy, and a path that scales across use cases.

  • Issue once Create a credential that travels with the holder.
  • Hold privately Data stays with the person, not trapped in your database.
  • Verify fast Partners check only what they need — in seconds.

Keep the complexity hidden

One quiet path from issue to verify. Your teams see outcomes — Credproof runs the hard parts underneath.
